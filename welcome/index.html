Bumpline is installed

The buttons appear inside Vinted, not here. Two steps.

  1. 1

    Pin Bumpline to the toolbar

    Click the puzzle piece beside the address bar, then the pin next to Bumpline. The icon then stays in view.

  2. 2

    Open your own wardrobe

    Every item of yours still for sale gets a Relist and a Relist as draft button. Nowhere else.

    The copy is saved as a draft before the old listing is deleted, so the item always exists somewhere. Both buttons delete the original; the draft one stops before publishing.
